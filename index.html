The Founding Partner Program
Real Commission, Not Just Free Product · Your Face, Your Rules · In the Room While It's Being Built

You're not an ad. You're a founder in this too.

So Sweet & Co is looking for a small first class of Founding Partners — creators who get paid real commission, keep full ownership of their content, and help shape the brand before the rest of the world ever sees it.

01 — The Vision

This brand is being built with its partners, not on top of them.

So Sweet & Co exists because the press-on nail category is full of brands that treat creators like billboards — post this, tag that, don't ask questions. We built the opposite.

"A Founding Partner isn't a promotional vendor. It's a strategic creative partner, in the room during the brand's formative stage — with a compensation structure that reflects it."
— The founding principle, Section 1

Being early means something here. The first class of Founding Partners will shape which sets get made permanent, which drops sell out, and which content actually resonates — that feedback loop goes both ways, not just brand-to-creator.

Everything downstream of that idea — the commission structure, the content ownership rules, the way disputes get resolved — is built to make this a partnership two people would actually want to be in, not a contract one side tolerates.

The goal is simple: a brand that's profitable for So Sweet & Co and genuinely good for the people building it alongside us.

07 — Where This Goes

The first 60 days are just the start.

The Initial Term is short on purpose — enough time to see real content, real sales, and real fit before anyone commits further. What happens next is up to both sides.

Option A
Month-to-Month
Keep going on a rolling 30-day basis. Either side can renew with 14 days' notice before each cycle — low commitment, steady partnership.
Option B
Extended Term
Negotiate a 6- or 12-month renewed agreement with updated terms — the move for a partner who's found a real rhythm with the brand.
The Big One
Option C
Founder Equity Conversion
By mutual agreement, restructure the relationship at renewal to include a revenue share or equity component — a real stake in what you helped build.
08 — Your Content, Your Rules

You keep your voice. You keep your work.

This is a non-exclusive partnership, and your content stays yours. Here's exactly where the lines are.

What You Can Still Do
  • ✓Work with any other beauty or lifestyle brand — this program is non-exclusive by design.
  • ✓Post organic, unpaid content about any product you personally use, without restriction.
  • ✕The only line: no paid partnership with a direct press-on nail competitor during your Term.

09 — Questions, Answered

The things everyone asks first.

No need to DM Tank about these — they're already settled.

Do I need to buy anything to join?+
No. Your onboarding kit is gifted, delivered before your first required post. There's no buy-in, no starter fee, nothing to purchase.
Am I an employee of So Sweet & Co?+
No — you're an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of the company in the legal sense. You're responsible for your own taxes on commissions earned, and a 1099 is issued if you cross IRS thresholds.
What if I don't hit the monthly content minimums?+
Life happens — reach out to Tank before the month closes if you're going to fall short. The minimums are what keeps the partnership active, so consistent, uncommunicated misses are treated differently than an occasional heads-up.
What if a sale doesn't get credited to me?+
Submit a dispute in writing within 30 days of the commission statement. The team reviews order data and responds within 5 business days — if it was a tracking error, the commission gets corrected in the next pay cycle.
Can I leave the program early?+
Yes. Either side can end the Agreement with 14 days' written notice. Any commission you've already earned still gets paid on the next regular cycle.
Can I still work with other brands?+
Yes — this is non-exclusive. The one restriction is paid work with a direct press-on nail competitor during your Term. Every other beauty or lifestyle brand is fair game, and organic content about any product is unrestricted.
